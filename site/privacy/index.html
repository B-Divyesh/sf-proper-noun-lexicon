Plain-language policy · Effective August 28, 2026

How Proper Noun Lexicon handles data.

Proper Noun Lexicon handles sensitive terminology without collecting it. The CLI reads and writes the local files you choose. The review desk performs corrections in your browser.

What stays on your device

Your approved terms, aliases, raw transcript draft, correction audit, and license token are stored in browser local storage on your device. Demo data uses separate keys beginning with demo:pnl: and is removed when you choose Start for real. Model-export files and audits are generated locally. We do not receive that content.

What is sent

When you buy or verify a license, your browser contacts the Sociobot billing API. It receives the license token and standard connection metadata needed to serve the request. Checkout is operated by Sociobot/Dodo as merchant of record and has its own checkout privacy notice. This site contains no analytics, tracking pixels, ad scripts, or third-party fonts.

Your controls

Export your vocabulary as CSV at any time. Remove individual terms in the review desk. To erase all locally saved workspace and license data, clear this site’s storage in your browser settings. Uninstalling the CLI removes no files you created; delete those files yourself when you no longer need them.

Offline cache

A service worker caches the application shell and original illustration so the local tools can reopen offline. It does not sync your content or contact lists.

Contact

Privacy questions can be filed in the project’s public issue tracker. Do not include private vocabulary or license tokens in an issue.
